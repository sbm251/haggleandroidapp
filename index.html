Haggl privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This privacy policy covers Haggl, the price comparison service at haggl.eu, and the Haggl app for Android (package com.haggl.eu). Haggl helps you compare prices for the same product across ten Amazon stores in Europe and then sends you to Amazon to buy.

1. Who we are

Haggl is run by Shane Marchant (sole trader), based in Malta. VAT number: MT3297-5608.

Privacy contact: support@haggl.eu

2. The Android app

The Haggl Android app shows the haggl.eu service and follows this policy. The app:

  • uses internet access and checks whether you are online (to show an offline screen);
  • may ask to send you notifications (Android 13 and later). This is optional and you can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings. If you allow notifications, they are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which gives your device a random notification token; we use it only to send you Haggl notifications;
  • does not read your contacts, location, photos, files or the other apps on your device;
  • does not use the Android advertising ID and contains no third-party advertising or tracking code. It may show Haggl's own promotions for deals on haggl.eu;
  • opens Amazon and other shop links in the Amazon app or your browser, not inside the Haggl app. Those sites have their own privacy policies;
  • does not require an account; your email address is only asked for if you choose to set a price alert.

3. Information we handle

  • Your preferences. Your language, delivery country, chosen stores and cookie choice are saved in your browser or app storage on your device so Haggl remembers them. They are not linked to your identity.
  • Price alerts. If you set a price alert, we store your email address, the product and your target price, and use them only to email you when the price drops below your target. Alerts you set are also listed on your device. Every alert email has an unsubscribe link that stops the alert.
  • Searches. The words you search for are sent to our servers to find prices. Recent search terms may be shown anonymously on the Haggl home page under "Recently searched"; they are never linked to your name or email.
  • Price votes. If you vote on a price, a small functional cookie (haggl_dv) and a hashed record of the vote are used to count votes and prevent duplicates. Votes and their hashes are kept for 12 months.
  • Affiliate clicks. When you open a link to an Amazon store, Haggl counts the click without using cookies.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages requested. This is used to run, secure and fix the service.
  • Emails you send us. If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.
  • Analytics. Non-essential analytics (such as Google Analytics) are only loaded if you allow analytics cookies. You can say no and Haggl works the same.

4. Amazon and other websites

As an Amazon Associate, Haggl earns from qualifying purchases. When you follow a link to Amazon, you leave Haggl and Amazon's own privacy policy and cookies apply. Haggl does not receive your Amazon account, payment or order details.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with anyone except service providers who help us host and run Haggl (for example, to send price alert emails), or where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep data

  • Preferences stay on your device until you clear them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Price alerts (email address, product and target price): until you unsubscribe, or ask us to delete them.
  • Price votes and their hashes: 12 months.
  • Server logs and analytics: only as long as needed to run the service and meet legal obligations.
  • Emails: as long as needed to deal with your message.

7. Security

All connections to haggl.eu, including from the app, use HTTPS encryption. Access to our systems is limited to what is needed to run Haggl.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Email support@haggl.eu and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the Maltese data protection authority (the Information and Data Protection Commissioner, idpc.org.mt) or your local authority.

You can delete everything stored on your device at any time by clearing the Haggl app's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

9. Children

Haggl is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
